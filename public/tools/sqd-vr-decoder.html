About these results

This decoder reads published manufacturer catalog-number conventions and returns the most likely configuration. It is an identification aid, not a specification: numbers get reused and revised, nameplates get mis-stamped, and some factory-fitted options never appear in a type designation at all. Confirm any result against the breaker’s own nameplate and the manufacturer’s documentation before ordering a part, fitting a replacement, or relying on a rating.

Power circuit breakers are hazardous equipment. Installation, testing and maintenance are work for qualified personnel following NFPA 70E and the equipment manufacturer’s instructions.
