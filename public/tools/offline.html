Keeping this copy current

  • The live tools at voytenmanuals.com/tools are the source of truth. Re-copy this folder after any decoder update rather than editing files here — an edited local copy will silently drift from the deployed one.
  • Keep the folder intact. Each decoder loads decoder-fonts.css, decoder-pdf.js, decoder-quote.js, jspdf.umd.min.js and the fonts/ folder from alongside it.
