Siemens WL / Sentron WL Decoder

Low Voltage Circuit Breaker — 15-Digit Catalog Number Decoder
Both listings share the same 15 digit positions but use different code sets for the class, frame, mounting and ampere digits. If you pick the wrong one, the decoder will tell you which to switch to.
Decodes all 15 positions — interrupting class, frame size, mounting, poles, frame ampere rating, rating plug, ETU trip unit and every factory accessory digit. Non-automatic switches are recognised automatically. The short 6-character stem that dealers list breakers under (e.g. WLL3F340) also decodes, as far as it goes.
